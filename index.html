About Ken Block

Ken Block (1967~2023)was a legendary driver who was very fierce and fast. He also is the co-founder of the Hoonigan Racing Division and the Gymkhana series, was called one of the best rally drivers in planet earth.

While he was the co-founder of many companies he did have some challenges. For example, when one of his builds were going on the Pikes Peak competition, the engine had a catastrophic engine failure forcing him to retire from the season.

Iconic quote

"Go fast, risk everything!"

His acheivements

  • 6 medals in WRC
  • 16 wins in Rally America
  • Created gymkhana
